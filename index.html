Impact Score — roughly the points a player generates minus the points he gives away. Scoring is weighted by effective field goal percentage, so 30 points on 20 shots counts for more than 30 points on 30 shots, and a three counts as a three. On top of that, award voting adds up to eight points: five for MVP, three for Defensive Player of the Year, scaled by the share of the vote a player won. The box score part still cannot tell who created a shot and who was set up for it, which flatters players fed easy baskets by someone else. And because the voting is now an input, this score can no longer be used to judge how well the formula predicts that voting.
